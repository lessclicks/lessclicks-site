Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Lessclicks Studio ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data for our mobile game, Survivors: New Frontier.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal identifying information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, or phone number. All game progression and save data are tied to your device or platform account (e.g., Apple Game Center) and securely synchronized through those official platform services.

2. Analytics and Crash Reports

We may collect anonymous gameplay telemetry and crash reports to improve game performance, stability, and balance. This data is strictly non-personal and cannot be used to identify you.

3. Children's Privacy

Survivors: New Frontier is designed to be family-friendly. We do not intentionally collect or solicit personal information from children under 13.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at: support@lessclicksstudio.com
